# Using Eight

A short guide to making a routine, followed by answers to common questions.

## Make your first routine

1. **Start.** On "Your routines", type a name, say how many dancers and how big the stage is, and press Create. Everyone starts in a line.
2. **Place the dancers.** Drag them where you want them. Drag a box around several, or Shift-click, to move a group. Under "Arrange everyone" on the right you can drop the group into a line, circle, V and other shapes, then turn, mirror, spread or tighten it.
3. **Add the next formation.** Press "Add formation" above the timeline. It starts as a copy of the one before, so you only move the people who change places. Faint ghosts show where each person came from.
4. **Set the timing.** Each formation is a coloured bar on the timeline. Drag its right edge to change how long it holds, and the small knob inside it to change how long everyone takes to walk in. You can also type the numbers on the right.
5. **Add music or a video.** Press "Add music or video". A song shows its waveform under the bars. A video plays beside the stage, in step with the formations.
6. **Play it.** Press Play or the Space bar. Use the Speed slider to slow it down and "Loop formation" to repeat one transition.
7. **Fix collisions.** If two dancers would walk into each other, the panel on the right names them and rings them in red. Select one, then drag the small white handle on their path to bend it around the other.
8. **Share it.** Press Export and choose a routine file, a picture, PDF sheets or a video.

### Things worth knowing early

- **Dancers:** click one to rename them or change their label, colour and shape. "Add prop" makes a box you can resize for a chair, riser or platform.
- **Counts or seconds:** under Routine on the right, "Time formations in" switches the whole routine between seconds and counts. Set the tempo and the counts in a bar there too.
- **Snapping:** "Snap to" above the timeline makes edges jump to counts, half counts, bars, whole seconds, or other formations. On the stage, "Dancers snap to" sets the step dancers jump by. Hold Alt while dragging to ignore snapping.
- **Right-click a formation** to duplicate it, reset it to the one before, line everyone up, move it or delete it.
- **Dancers' view** flips the stage so you see it from the back, the way the dancers face. "Mirror video" does the same kind of flip for a video.
- **Undo** is Ctrl or Cmd + Z. It goes back as far as when you opened the routine.

## Questions

### Where are my routines saved?

In your browser, on the device you are using. Eight saves by itself a fraction of a second after every change; there is no Save button. Nothing is sent to a server, because there isn't one.

### There are no accounts. What does that mean for me?

It means your routines belong to one browser on one device, not to you as a person. In practice:

- **Another device or browser shows nothing.** Your laptop's Chrome, your laptop's Safari and your phone each have their own separate list.
- **A private or incognito window forgets everything** when you close it.
- **Clearing your browser's site data or history deletes your routines.** So does uninstalling the browser.
- **Safari may delete them by itself** if you do not open Eight for about a week. Adding Eight to your Home Screen or Dock avoids this.
- **A browser very short of disk space may clear them.** Eight asks the browser to keep them, but the browser has the final say.
- **Nobody can recover a lost routine for you.** There is no copy anywhere else.

The protection against all of these is the same: export what you care about.

### How do I back up my work?

- **One routine:** Export → Routine file. You get a small `.eight.json` file.
- **Everything:** on "Your routines", press "Back up all". You get one file holding every routine.

Keep those files wherever you keep other important files. "Import" reads either kind back in.

### How do I move a routine to another device, or send it to someone?

Export it as a routine file, send the file however you like (chat, email, AirDrop), and on the other device press Import. The music or video does not travel inside the file, so add it again on the other device.

### How do I share with dancers who won't use Eight?

Export PDF sheets for something to print or pin up, or a video for the group chat. Neither needs Eight to open.

### Can two of us work on the same routine at once?

Not yet. Each person has their own copy. If you both edit and swap files, one version replaces the other; nothing is merged. Live sharing is planned.

### What if I open the same routine in two tabs?

Avoid it. Each tab saves the whole routine when you edit, so whichever tab you edited last overwrites the other's changes.

### Is my music or video uploaded anywhere?

No. It stays in your browser on your device, the same as your routines.

### What files can I add, and how big?

Songs as MP3, WAV or M4A, up to 60 MB. Videos as MP4, MOV or WebM, as large as your device has room for. A routine holds one song or one video; to use several songs, join them into one file first.

### My video has no waveform. Why?

Either the video is over 300 MB or its sound could not be read. It still plays and stays in step. Under Routine, press "Use a song file for the waveform" and pick the song; if the music starts a few seconds into the video, scrub to that point and press "Starts at playhead".

### Why does exporting a video take so long?

It records the routine as it plays, so a three-minute routine takes three minutes. Keep the tab open and in front until the "Save" link appears. For a long routine, choose 720p.

### I exported, and nothing downloaded.

Look for a download prompt or a blocked-download icon in the browser's address bar, and check your Downloads folder. For a video, press the "Save" link in the message that appears when recording finishes.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes, once you have opened it one time while online. After that it opens and saves offline.

### Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It opens and you can look through a routine, but it is built for a laptop and has had little testing on touch screens. Right-click menus are not available on iPhone and iPad, and the volume slider has no effect there.

### The site moved to a new address and my routines are gone.

Browsers keep saved data separately for each web address. Go back to the old address, use "Back up all", then Import that file at the new address.

### Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no payment, no ads and no tracking.

### Whose responsibility is the music in an exported video?

Yours. Eight puts the song you added into the video you export. If the song is someone else's, the usual rules for posting it apply.
